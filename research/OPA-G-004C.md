## C1: electronic scene and independent oracle

Freeze deterministic synthetic triangle scenes, stable primitive IDs, binary AABB BVH and ray batches before evaluation; publish full inputs, node topology, hashes, seeds and tie semantics. Include distributed triangles, clustered/overlapping boxes, degenerate triangles (defined as no surface hit), shared edges, tangent/parallel rays, inside origins, misses, near-equal distances and anisotropic scenes. Use represented dyadic inputs where feasible. Closed forward ray t>=0; double-sided triangles, closed barycentric boundary, coplanar rays explicitly outside the initial triangle-intersection contract (report exclusions, never silently count as misses). Exact rational noncoplanar ray-triangle brute force defines closest hit, ties by smallest primitive ID. Independently validate a realizable compiled FP64 triangle and box reference; preserve every discrepancy and exclude unmatched methods from performance comparisons. Validate BVH containment, leaf coverage, topology, deterministic order and no cycles. Initially use fixed depth-first child order, no nearest-distance pruning; any later pruning is a separate matched experiment. Compare exact brute force, exact BVH and compiled electronic BVH, including hit identity, distance error and node/triangle counts; no optical claim in C1.

## C2: frozen hybrid BVH

Use new disjoint scene/ray evaluation and separate calibration geometry. Freeze candidate max_min_125, factor 1.25, optical model, five scenarios and ten independently seeded repeats as in G-004B2; fit gain/offset and maximum minimum-side residual only on calibration, never BVH evaluation. Same normalized operand quantization and bias drift assumptions. Do not select alternative thresholds or scenario-dependent modes after seeing results. Electronic preguards and compiled FP64 fallback must retain the exact represented-input contract; independently audit them for every visited node, including nodes reached only by erroneous traversal. Exact oracle is validation only, not free fallback. Use shared deterministic per-ray/node noise keyed by scenario/repeat/ray/node (and side index), independent of traversal order and compared policies. Count every evaluated side, guard, fallback, leaf and triangle, including wasted tests. Preserve raw optical and after-fallback classifications; compare pure electronic, all-optical fallback, raw optical diagnostic and frozen hybrid. A counterfactual exact per-node label identifies FP/FN; distinguish extra/missing visits against electronic reference and actual omitted nearest hits. Report final nearest-hit ID, hit/miss, distance against brute-force oracle, per-scene and repeat. Distinguish pair-level errors from scene-level errors; an incorrect node can be masked at final output. Report exact nearest-hit identity and explicit distance tolerance fixed before evaluation (e.g. absolute + relative bound with documented units). No performance ranking unless all compared methods satisfy zero observed final miss/false-hit/identity errors and distance gate on every panel/repeat; also report strict node-level zero-error gate separately. Any failure remains an archived result, not a reason to tune on test data.

Distance comparison uses ray parameter t (not Euclidean length), absolute and relative tolerances 1e-10, exact nearest primitive ID with smallest-ID ties. Any nondegenerate coplanar ray/triangle pair excludes the entire ray from nearest-hit comparisons; exclusions listed. All triangles and nodes audited outside traversal work counts; preserve discrepancies, visited and accepted node IDs and tested primitive IDs.
