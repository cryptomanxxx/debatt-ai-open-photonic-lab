Debatt-AI Open Photonic Accelerator (OPA) 0.24
Experimental photonic integrated circuit (PIC) design package
Prepared: 2026-11-09

Included files:
- opa_p_four_facet_ports_exploratory.gds
- opa_p_four_facet_ports_manifest.json

Design concept:
Passive 2x2 Mach-Zehnder interferometer (MZI) based on the Luxtelligence
lnoi400 photonic process design kit (PDK), with two nominal optical inputs,
two nominal optical outputs, inverse tapers and waveguide fanout to opposite
edges of an assumed 10 mm x 5 mm die. A nominal 127 um fiber pitch is used.

Internal checks performed:
- GDSII export and KLayout readability
- Four labeled nominal optical facet ports
- Port coordinates at the two nominal die-edge x positions
- Two distinct external optical rail regions at eight transverse sections

IMPORTANT FABRICATION STATUS:
THIS IS NOT A FOUNDRY-APPROVED OR TAPE-OUT-READY DESIGN.
The GDSII is an actual lithographic layout artifact but has NOT passed the
fabrication provider's official design rule check (DRC). The fiber pitch,
coupler positions, chip-edge processing, packaging, optical losses,
transmission matrix and electrical/system integration are not validated.
The GDSII must not be submitted to production without provider review.

Purpose of sharing with EuroCDP:
Evidence of practical independent photonic chip-design activity and a
specific starting point for requesting technical assessment, access to
design resources and prototyping advice. No manufacturing order is placed.
