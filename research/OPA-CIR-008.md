## Architecture and accounting boundary

Reuse exact CIR007 ZIP (artifact 14528427171, SHA256 abc07f6aa4bc74b2dbb708a3cbeb29a23ee557bfd724259baa5eb45d0202e26c); independently recompute all 36 admission decisions from 180 cases. Model one 4x4 complex SVD tile, 12 MZIs, 24 MMIs, 32 phase control parameters, four ideal attenuators; 8 I/Q DACs, 8 driver lanes, 4 receiver hybrids, 16 photodiodes, 8 balanced differential front ends, 8 ADCs. One vector per symbol at conditional 10 Gsymbol/s (consistent with CIR007's 7 GHz noise-bandwidth proxy). 16 complex MAC per vector, no conversion into real TOPS or GPU comparison.

Reference planes: maximum aggregate signal at core entrance after encoding, LO total at each ideal hybrid, detected signal from CIR007 (no second deduction for internal mesh or singular-value loss). Upstream source coupling, two splitter excess stages and signal modulator loss use existing [AG003 parameter assumptions](../antigravity/reproduction/AG-PHOTONIC-003/original/params.py), not new verified specs. Fixed equal splitter provisions four branches for a full-scale component, hence 4x aggregate signal bound; adaptive allocation is an optimistic 1x scenario, no implementation claimed. LO source provisions 4x LO per hybrid with excess loss and coupling, not an additional ideal split factor. Separate signal and LO optical source powers divided by assumed wall-plug efficiency; phase locking unspecified. No modeled optical amplification.

## Frozen sensitivity grid and checks

Four passing settings x low/nominal/high AG003 assumptions x two signal allocation cases x 8/16 TIA front-end counts x SRAM/HBM payload memory = 96 rows. Conditional static TIA and calibration proxy; converter energy scales by 2^(bits-8) for both DAC and ADC, explicitly a sensitivity assumption. Eight driver lanes per symbol. Packed payload 16 real quadrature values per vector (input+output), at nominal bits, excludes metadata/weights. Cooling multiplier applied to partial subtotal. Baseline fixed splitter, 8 front ends, SRAM, nominal historical parameters. Report source power, all partial electrical terms, inherited error, detector max, conditional energy/vector and energy/complex MAC. Verify source and admission identities, grid, conservation, accounting, paired splitter and TIA scaling, scenario ordering, finite outputs. No new experimental precision threshold; inherited screen is not application accuracy.

This is **partial accounting**, not complete power or validated feasibility: actual phase hold and attenuator controls, external routing/output/hybrid losses, phase tracking, digital normalization/output correction, activation, calibration probes, weight updates, idle/clock/host and measured timing remain unknown. Proxy cooling is not a thermal design. No actual converter part selection, ENOB, speed, energy advantage or physical test. Additional output loss can invalidate the inherited screen; no implicit compensation. No WDM parallelism.

After CI and review, consolidate CIR001–008 evidence in a final report, permanently archive exact artifacts, then prioritize system bottlenecks rather than endless isolated sweeps. Preserve WDM as unresolved, electronic activation as baseline, and fabrication roadmap.
