# OPA-CIR-007 — single-carrier coherent electronic/optical interface

CIR006 [run 44517897095](https://github.com/cryptomanxxx/debatt-ai-open-photonic-lab/actions/runs/44517897095) passed five numerical checks and 255 cases, but only the single center carrier passed the 5% matrix screen for all targets. Even per-channel complex gain gives worst three-channel errors 13.0657%, 24.9476%, 47.3219% at 25/50/100 GHz. Do not assume WDM throughput. Use 1550 nm baseline; defer WDM until system bottleneck review. This does not prove all WDM architectures infeasible.

Use the exact CIR006 ZIP and nested CIR005 ZIP, verify hashes and reconstruct the frozen central complex transfer and held-out vectors; no new optical weight calibration. Reuse OPA-SYS-003's balanced coherent shot/TIA concepts, but explicitly model a four-diode I/Q hybrid rather than the previous single-quadrature 50:50 illustration. R=0.9 A/W and 15 pA/sqrt(Hz) effective difference-pair TIA density are conditional AG003 assumptions; 7 GHz rectangular noise bandwidth from the existing 10 Gsymbol/s proxy, not a rate prediction. No physical device selection. Input vector normalization and quantized drive headroom corrected digitally. Ideal linear signed I/Q field encoding; no assumed modulator transfer or driver energy. Four detector powers |E +/- LO|^2/4 and |E +/- iLO|^2/4; total LO per hybrid, sum power conserved, difference current R sqrt(Plo) E. Independent pair shot variance q R(Ps+Plo) B plus TIA variance. Gaussian approximation, no RIN, imbalance, saturation or phase tracking. Report max diode power for future limits, not hardware feasibility.

Frozen grid: five nonzero CIR005 targets, 32 held-out vectors, 64 paired Gaussian noise repeats, equal nominal DAC/ADC bits 4/6/8/10, maximum aggregate signal launch 0.01/0.1/1 mW, LO per output 0.1/1/3 mW, 180 groups. Four output hybrids use 4x LO power. ADC full scale 1.2 R sqrt(Plo Plaunch) fixed before data; report clipping. Compare core, DAC-only, noiseless ADC and noisy ADC errors; preserve negative results. Provisional worst-target 5% relative vector error and no clipping screen is separate from CI, not ENOB or accuracy. Verify center and core reproduction, hybrid identities, passivity, finite complete grid, LO accounting. No measured hardware, throughput, electrical energy or WDM claim. CIR008 will integrate conditional system accounting and identify missing hardware inputs. Permanent archive of prior artifacts remains necessary.
