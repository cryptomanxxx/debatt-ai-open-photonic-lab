# OPA-LIB-005-C — Exact PDK source discovery, not a measurement match

This phase checked the same pinned SiEPIC EBeam upstream revision as LIB001 (`363fbb52eefa8669100499ab531d3fe7befc4867`), including the exact `ebeam_bdc_te1550` and `ebeam_gc_te1550` families. [Source inventory and Git blobs](../components/comparison/lib005-source-discovery.json) lists seven read-only upstream files, including LUT maps, `opics_ebeam` source and component documentation. CI fetches only these revision-pinned public files and verifies Git blob identities and parsable structure. No source data are republished by this PR.

**High-value discovery:** The official upstream PDK contains a 220-nm-height, 500-nm-width four-port BDC model `bdc_Thickness =220 width=500.sparam`, with 16 ordered port pair blocks × 51 optical frequency samples. Each entry stores frequency, magnitude, and phase (units and reference planes need further verification). The `opics_ebeam` class links `ebeam_bdc_te1550` to `bdc_lookup_table.xml`. It also links the GC compact model to `GC_TE_lookup_table.xml`; nominal 220-nm, zero-delta GC file is `GC_TE1550_thickness=220 deltaw=0.txt`, 100 rows and 9 numeric columns. Its column semantics and equivalence to exact archived GDS remain unverified. The PDK's documentation displays published experimental figures and discusses FDTD/experimental modeling, but **does not establish that these particular coefficient files are raw measurements of exactly the archived GDS cells**.

Official upstream documentation: [BDC](https://github.com/SiEPIC/SiEPIC_EBeam_PDK/blob/363fbb52eefa8669100499ab531d3fe7befc4867/docs/components/ebeam/ebeam_bdc/ebeam_bdc.md) | [Grating coupler](https://github.com/SiEPIC/SiEPIC_EBeam_PDK/blob/363fbb52eefa8669100499ab531d3fe7befc4867/docs/components/ebeam/grating_coupler/grating_coupler.md) | [exact BDC model](https://github.com/SiEPIC/SiEPIC_EBeam_PDK/blob/363fbb52eefa8669100499ab531d3fe7befc4867/klayout/EBeam/CML/EBeam/source_data/bdc_TE_source/bdc_Thickness%20%3D220%20width%3D500.sparam) | [exact GC data](https://github.com/SiEPIC/SiEPIC_EBeam_PDK/blob/363fbb52eefa8669100499ab531d3fe7befc4867/klayout/EBeam/CML/EBeam/source_data/gc_source/GC_TE1550_thickness%3D220%20deltaw%3D0.txt).

## Other investigated leads and negative matches

- SiEPIC `ebeam_dc_te1550` model files and [issue #321](https://github.com/SiEPIC/SiEPIC_EBeam_PDK/issues/321) refer to a **different directional coupler** than the archived **broadband** `ebeam_bdc_te1550`. Do not transfer this model between cells.
- [SiEPIC openEBL program](https://siepic.ca/openebl/) offers open designs and historic measurement metadata. An exact original cell hash, fabrication run, curve and reuse rights have not yet been established for our archived cells.
- [Luxtelligence official model guide](https://luxtelligence.github.io/lxt_pdk_gf/notebooks/models.html) describes a combination of measured characterization and FDTD, but does not identify which particular coefficients in our pinned `mmi_2x2_optimized_1550` file arose from which actual device/run. Our prior LIB003 S-parameter coefficients remain **simulation/model evidence only**; 201/201 nonpassive directional-coupler samples remain quarantined.
- [Twente TFLN source](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14065933) is an independently fabricated and measured **different** TFLN process. No direct calibration or complex optical S is available for pinned lnoi400.

## Decision

At the end of this discovery audit **zero new exact-revision, exact-geometry, matched raw experimental measurement datasets** have been verified. Two candidate *numerical* PDK files are newly located, not imported or used for circuit calibration. Next: independently audit the BDC model's complex port/order/phase and wavelength response and verify reference plane and geometry provenance before deciding whether to import it in a separate LIB005-D numerical PR. In parallel seek matching original measurement provenance from SiEPIC or perform bounded passive physical tests from LIB005-B. Do not declare LIB005 complete or approve FAB001 fabrication without facility, access, DRC and measurement gates.
