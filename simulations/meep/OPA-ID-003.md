# OPA-ID-003 — Fabrication-tolerant directional coupler

Compare nominal-only and robust parametric inverse design under a uniform guide-width bias. Unlike ID-002, this is a binary two-material geometry search, not continuous-density adjoint optimization. The model is generic 2D Ez with index 1.8 in air, not the previously studied 3D silicon-on-insulator coupler (OPA-COUPLER-003). Nominal port width 600 nm, separated port centerlines ±1.1 µm, cosine approach/exit bends of 2.5 µm each. The search spans 12 candidates: interaction lengths 2, 4, 6, 8 µm and nominal gaps 200, 350, 500 nm.

At 1550 nm and 30 px/µm each candidate is evaluated at full-width offsets 0 and ±20 nm with fixed centerlines (positive width bias reduces gap). Each input is normalized against its own straight-waveguide reference. Objective error is max(|Tbar−0.5|, |Tcross−0.5|), where powers are fractions of incident mode power, penalizing both loss and imbalance. Nominal selection minimizes nominal error; robust selection minimizes worst training error with deterministic tie-breaking. Identical candidate pool and objective. No claim that the global optimum is found.

Both selected designs are independently re-simulated at 30 and 60 px/µm for training conditions and held-out ±10/±30 nm width offsets at 1550 nm, and nominal width at 1530/1570 nm. Held-out results do not select the design. Measure forward band-1 power in both output guides and backward band-1 power in both input guides; subtract incident reference DFT fields at the excited input. Report total transmission, split fraction, split error, reflection and unaccounted power. Finite modal span and evanescent overlap may bias measurement. Only one input is excited: no full scattering matrix or phase verification. Width offsets are vertical, not exact normal etch offsets on curved sidewalls. No thickness, sidewall, dispersion or statistical yield model.

Mandatory checks cover complete data, finite measurements, normalization, passivity sanity and search selection. Research targets separately report robust superiority on fine-resolution training/holdout sets, distinct geometry, split error below five percentage points, >95% transmission, <1% reflection, and resolution convergence. Negative results are legitimate; no targets are assumed achieved.

Outputs: original JSON report, search and validation CSVs and locked environment. The geometry is reproducible from the reported parameters and implementation. No physical fabrication, foundry validation or novelty claim.
