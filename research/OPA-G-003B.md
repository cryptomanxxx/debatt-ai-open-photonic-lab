# OPA-G-003B — Optical Boundary Precision and Robust Readout

## Question

Can a ray-level confidence window reduce electronic fallback relative to the G-003 any-side window, without hiding false negatives? G-003's near_face set was entirely handled by electronic guards; the combined scenario fell back on 5477/9108 optical-stage rays and still had four false positives. See [G-003 analysis](../results/opa-g-003/README.md) and [original archive](../results/ci-originals/opa-g-003/45810482156-15111277146/archive-provenance.json). No empirical hardware tolerances or safety guarantees are inferred.

## Method

Reuse G-003 four-MZM field and balanced receiver, G-001 rational face oracle, and G-002 signed determinant. No new PDK cell or process assumption. Construct dyadic boxes and rays with entry at t=1 on one axis and exit at t=1-delta/w on another. Delta is zero or +/-2^-k for k=4,8,12,16,24,40; randomize widths, shifts, reflections and axis order, with two or three active axes. Every boundary sample must reach optical computation. Compare exact normalized determinant minima, float64 minima, raw optical minima, gain-only and affine-calibrated minima on identical receiver samples. The exact oracle is on represented inputs, not an arbitrary-real model. Separately audit guard-only classifications on G-001 workloads.

Use independent seeded geometric calibration and held-out draws (128 per signed displacement or zero stratum before active-axis split); remove any overlapping represented rays from held-out data before evaluation. Fit affine scalar gain/offset on 8192 independent uniform operand samples as in G-003. Compare empirical q99 absolute uniform-side residual with q99 absolute geometric ray-minimum residual from calibration rays only. Four illustrative settings: scale 0.15 and 0.6 rad alone, noise std 0.005 alone, and G-003 combined bias/phase/loss/noise setting. Three independent seeded repeats. No tuning on held-out labels.

Compare the historical any-side ambiguity rule (any |side| <= q) with minimum-window ambiguity (|min(side)| <= q). With two thresholds per side at -q and +q, a confident negative side rejects, all confident positive sides accept, and otherwise exact electronic fallback is required. This is equivalent to minimum-window logic except threshold equality is deliberately ambiguous unless another side is below -q. The simulation computes normalized analog values and aggregates electronically; it does not implement a physical comparator or ADC. The window is symmetric and empirical, not a simultaneous, distribution-free or guaranteed error bound. Any-side and minimum-window policies can trade errors against fallback differently; neither is assumed superior.

## Gate and outputs

Numerical checks: exact determinant and guard classifications agree with independent face oracle; exact tangencies and both nonzero margin signs; all boundary rays reach optics; float margin agreement; disjoint calibration/evaluation inputs; both active-axis counts and all strata retained; finite calibration, subset of fallback selections and count invariants. Epsilon-window exact fallback is a numerical diagnostic only. No required accuracy target for nonideal optics: negative results are valid. Report FP/FN separately by displacement sign, exponent and axis count, optical ray and side counts, fallback and held-out ray-margin coverage; coverage repeated across rows is aggregate per scenario/repeat/threshold, not independent samples. Compare policies on identical noise realizations, not aggregate repeated thresholds as independent observations. Runtime is not hardware performance.
